## Store listing

**Category:** Productivity → Tools (or "Workflow & Planning")
**Language:** English

### Description

Hi. I have 347 tabs open.

If that sounds familiar, welcome. Tab Rehab is recovery for chronic tab hoarders. One tab at a time.

You don't keep tabs open because you like clutter. You keep them because you're afraid of losing them, and because half of them are secretly a to-do list. Tab Rehab answers that fear: everything you close can be undone, parked, snoozed or turned into a to-do. Nothing gets lost.

WHAT IT DOES
• See every tab in one dashboard, grouped by site or window. Search with "/".
• Rehab session: one tab at a time, oldest first. Keep (K), To-do (T), Snooze (S), Bookmark (B), Park (P) or Close (C).
• Quick wins: close duplicates, empty tabs, old searches and error pages in one click.
• Stale tabs: find what you haven't opened in 1 to 30 days and park it all at once.
• Park: save tabs to a list and close them. Restore them later, back into the window they came from.
• Snooze: close a tab now and have it reopen by itself tonight, tomorrow or next week.
• To-dos: turn "I kept this open so I don't forget" into a note with a reminder.
• Bookmarks: save tabs worth keeping for good, in the folder you choose.
• Recovery: your own progress. "You started at 347. Today: 41." and "You've let go of 812 tabs. You needed 6 back."
• Tab count on the toolbar icon (green, amber, red), right-click to park or save as to-do, Alt+Shift+T to open.

SAFETY NET
• Undo after every close, park or to-do.
• "Recently closed" log of everything Tab Rehab closed.
• Asks before parking more than 10 tabs.
• Export and import a JSON backup any time.

PRIVACY
What happens in rehab stays in rehab. Everything is stored on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no telemetry. Tab Rehab never sends your tabs, URLs or anything else anywhere.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Free and open source: github.com/jorgesousa/tab-rehab

## Privacy practices

**Single purpose:**
Tab Rehab helps people with too many open tabs review, close, save and reduce them safely: a dashboard of all tabs with ways to park, snooze, bookmark, turn tabs into to-dos and undo every action.

**Permission justifications:**
- **tabs:** Lists all open tabs (title, URL, last accessed time) in the dashboard so the user can review, search, group, close, park and restore them. Also shows the tab count on the toolbar badge.
- **storage:** Saves the user's parked tab lists, snoozed tabs, to-dos, settings and daily tab counts locally on the device.
- **unlimitedStorage:** Parked lists and the "Recently closed" safety net can grow to thousands of tabs for heavy users; this prevents data loss when the default local storage quota is reached.
- **alarms:** Reopens snoozed tabs at the chosen time, fires to-do reminders and runs a once-a-day check for stale tabs.
- **notifications:** Shows to-do reminders, tells the user when snoozed tabs come back, and an optional daily "you have N stale tabs" nudge (can be turned off).
- **contextMenus:** Adds "Save this tab as a to-do" and "Park this tab" to the right-click menu.
- **favicon:** Shows each site's icon next to tabs in the dashboard so they are easy to recognise.
- **bookmarks:** Saves tabs or parked lists as bookmarks in a folder the user chooses, only when the user clicks "Bookmark".

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. All JavaScript is in the package.

**Data usage:** Tick nothing collected. (Web history / website content stay on the device and are never transmitted, so they are not "collected" under the Web Store definition.)
Certify all three: not sold to third parties, not used for unrelated purposes, not used for creditworthiness/lending.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://jorgesousa.github.io/tab-rehab/privacy.html
